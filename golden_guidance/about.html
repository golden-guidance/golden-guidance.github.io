OUR COMPANY

A practical partner for building care.

Golden Guidance General Maintenance – L.L.C – S.P.C is based in Musaffah, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. Our listed activity is Buildings Maintenance.

We aim to help clients maintain the condition, usability, and appearance of their properties with a service approach built around professionalism, communication, and attention to detail.

We believe every property deserves thoughtful care and every client deserves a clear, respectful service experience.

Our Mission

To provide dependable building maintenance services with care, efficiency, and a commitment to client satisfaction.

Our Vision

To grow as a trusted maintenance service provider through lasting relationships and consistent service quality.

Our Values

Reliability, integrity, professionalism, attention to detail, and respect for our clients and their properties.
